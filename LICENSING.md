# Linda Probe licensing in one page

Linda Probe is one product that you can get in two ways. The rules are the same for both.

| | What you get | Where from |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows app** | Fluent 2.0 desktop window, instant filename search, offline OCR, semantic & photo search | `LindaProbe-Setup-2.0.0.exe` on [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/Lendarixon/Linda-Probe/releases/latest) or [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com/detail/9PGM1V68193X) |
| **Command line / Python** | the `linda_probe` package and CLI (`python -m linda_probe`, `linda-probe`) | [GitHub](https://github.com/Lendarixon/Linda-Probe) |
| **OCR & embedding models** | our OCR recognition model (`linda-ocr`) and open-source ONNX models | [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Lindarixon/linda-ocr); downloaded by the app when enabled |

## 1. Free: personal, non-commercial use

The Free edition (instant filename search, exact-word full-text search, file explorer, previews, export, citations, manual tags, duplicate finder) and the source code are **free** for personal, non-commercial use under **CC BY-NC 4.0** ([LICENSE](LICENSE)).

Our own OCR recognition model (`Lindarixon/linda-ocr`) is published separately on Hugging Face under **Apache-2.0**.

## 2. Commercial and Pro licence: what you buy

Commercial use (paid work or clients, use inside a company, school, or organization, or in a product/service you sell) and all Pro features (hybrid AI semantic search, offline OCR for scans/PDFs, photo search, automatic local tags) require a paid or gift licence key, an active 7-day offline trial, or an active temporary promo.

| Licence | Price | Who | Devices | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Personal** | $10, one-off | 1 named user | up to 3 | purchased version & fixes perpetually |
| **Team** | $30, one-off | up to 10 named users | up to 20 | purchased version & fixes perpetually |
| **Organization** | $500 per year | 1 organization at 1 site | up to 200 | all updates during the paid year |

- **7-day offline trial & promo keys:** both work offline, send nothing to Polar, and grant Personal Pro rights through the displayed UTC expiry date (one trial per Windows user and machine).
- **Purchased keys:** activated online via Polar (receives the key and a Probe-specific hashed machine ID, never document contents). Use **Remove key** while online before moving devices.
- **14-day full refund:** write to [lindapro.support@proton.me](mailto:lindapro.support@proton.me) or Polar.
- **Linda Detector vs Linda Probe:** Probe is a separate app and key; a Linda Detector key does not unlock Probe.
